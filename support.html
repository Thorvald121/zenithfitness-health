App Support

Support for Zenith Health & Fitness

Need help with meal logging, workouts, goals, body stats, or app behavior? Send a support request with the details below.

Email Support

Contact support for help with the Zenith Health & Fitness app. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version if available, and a short description of the issue.

Email support@zenithhealthandfit.com

Typical response time: within 24–48 hours.

Helpful Details to Include

  • The screen you were using
  • What you were trying to do
  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • Screenshots, if helpful

Common Questions

Quick help topics

How do I report a bug?

Email support with the screen you were on, the action you took, what happened, and any screenshots that may help.

How do I request a feature?

Send a short explanation of the feature and how it would improve your workout, nutrition, goal tracking, or progress tracking workflow.

What should I include in a support request?

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version if available, the issue you are seeing, and the steps that caused it.

Where is the privacy policy?

The privacy policy is available at zenithhealthandfit.com/privacy.html.
