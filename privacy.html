Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Overview

Zenith Health & Fitness respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the Zenith Health & Fitness mobile application or website.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may allow you to enter fitness, nutrition, progress, habit, or account-related information. This may include meals, calories, workouts, goals, body stats, progress notes, and related app activity.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide app functionality, help users track fitness and nutrition activity, improve the user experience, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support requests.

Support Communications

If you contact support, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. This information is used to respond to your request.

Analytics and Diagnostics

The app or website may use analytics or diagnostic tools to understand general usage, identify crashes, and improve performance. These tools may collect technical information such as device type, app version, operating system version, and general usage events.

Health and Fitness Disclaimer

Zenith Health & Fitness is intended for general fitness and nutrition tracking. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users should consult a qualified health professional before making major changes to diet, exercise, or health routines.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers necessary to operate the app, provide support, analyze performance, comply with legal obligations, or protect the app and its users.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

Children’s Privacy

Zenith Health & Fitness is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact
